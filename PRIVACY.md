# Feely Privacy Policy

_Effective 2 October 2026_

Feely is a mood journal for iPhone, made by Tomas Vopat. It keeps your
entries on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no
tracking.

## What stays on your phone

Everything you record — scores, tags, notes, settings and your check-in
history — is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. We never receive it.

- **iCloud Backup.** If you use iCloud Backup, iOS includes Feely's data in
  your device backup, as it does for most apps. That backup is between you and
  Apple; we can't access it.
- **AI summary.** On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, the weekly and monthly
  summary is written by Apple's on-device model from your entries and notes.
  It runs entirely on your iPhone: nothing is sent to us, to Apple's servers
  or anywhere else.
- **Reminders** are scheduled on your phone; no server is involved.
- **Exports.** CSV export and JSON backup create a file only when you ask, and
  you choose where it goes through the iOS share sheet.

## Apple Health (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Apple Health sync, Feely writes each entry's score to the Health
app as a State of Mind sample. Only the score and time are written — never
your tags or notes. Feely does not read any data from Health.

Once in Health, that data is managed by Apple's Health app and its settings,
including whether Health syncs it to iCloud. Turning sync off stops new writes;
you can delete what was written in the Health app. Feely never uses Health data
for advertising or shares it with anyone.

## What we receive: feedback you choose to send

The only information that ever leaves the app for us is a message you write
and send from **Settings → Feedback**. Nothing is sent until you tap Send. With
your message we receive:

- the app version, build and code version,
- your device model and iOS version,
- the app's language.

This is sent through [Web3Forms](https://web3forms.com), a form service that
forwards it to us by email. It isn't linked to any account or identifier, and
we use it only to read your feedback and fix problems. We keep these emails as
long as they're useful for that, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.
Please don't include sensitive personal details in a message.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings → Reset App** permanently deletes all entries and settings on
  your phone. So does deleting the app.
- Data written to Apple Health is deleted in the Health app.
- To have a feedback message deleted, email us.

## Children

Feely is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect
information from them.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR), you can ask what
data we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. In practice, the
only data we hold is feedback you sent. Contact us using the address below.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date
above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
